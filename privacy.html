Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to DataFlow.ai ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Intelligent Document Processing platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and company details
  • Document Data: Uploaded documents, extracted data, and processing history
  • Payment Information: Billing details processed securely through Razorpay/Stripe
  • Usage Data: Pages processed, credits used, and feature usage statistics
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and access logs

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve our document processing services
  • Process your payments and maintain your credit balance
  • Send you service updates, invoices, and support communications
  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance our platform
  • Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: All documents are encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Access Controls: Strict authentication and authorization mechanisms
  • Regular Audits: Periodic security assessments and vulnerability scanning
  • Secure Infrastructure: Hosted on enterprise-grade cloud platforms

5. Data Retention

Important: We automatically delete your processed documents and extracted data based on your plan:

  • Starter Plan: 30 days after processing
  • Pro Plan: 90 days after processing
  • Business Plan: 180 days after processing

Account information and transaction history are retained as required by law (typically 7 years for financial records).

6. Data Processing Partners

We use the following trusted third-party services:

  • Google Vision API: For OCR text extraction (subject to Google's privacy policy)
  • Google Gemini: For AI-powered data extraction (subject to Google's privacy policy)
  • Razorpay/Stripe: For secure payment processing
  • Supabase: For database hosting and authentication
  • DigitalOcean: For cloud infrastructure

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Portability: Export your data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@dataflow.ai

8. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or through the platform. Continued use of our services after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
